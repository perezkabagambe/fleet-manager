Fleet Manager

Privacy notice

Fleet Manager is a small private web app used to keep track of a group of cars: their details, issues, repairs, maintenance, purchased parts, costs and reminders.

What we store

  • About people: your name and email address, when you asked for an account, whether you are approved, and when you signed in. Nothing else. There are no passwords.
  • About cars: the details, photos and documents people upload for each car, and the record of who changed what and when.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored privately in the Google account that runs the app (Google Drive and Google Sheets). Nothing is stored in this public website, and nothing is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Who can see it

Only people who have been approved by an administrator and have signed in. Administrators can see who has an account.

Sign-in

You sign in with a one-time link or code sent by email, or with “Sign in with Google”. Google sign-in is used only to confirm your email address; we do not receive your Google password or access to your Google data.

Emails

The app sends emails to approved people only: sign-in links, approval messages and maintenance reminders.

Your choices

You can ask an administrator at any time to see, correct or delete your account details, or to stop your emails, by writing to potentiafleetmanager@gmail.com.
